**Gap:** The slides don't mention the "conflict types methodology" which is a major contribution.
**From thesis:** "The development of a methodology for developing propagator inference checkers... studying the conflict types of a particular propagation algorithm"
**Impact:** This is presented as the first main contribution but is completely absent from slides.

### 2. **Insufficient Context for "Individual Steps"**
**Gap:** The audience may not understand WHY checking individual steps is harder than checking whole proofs.
**From thesis:** Proofs consist of sequences of steps that can depend on previous steps, requiring global state management.
**Impact:** The research question motivation could be stronger.

### 3. **Missing SAT Context** 
**Gap:** No mention of SAT solver success story that motivated this work.

**Impact:** Audience may not appreciate the significance or precedent.

### 4. **Missing LCG Context**
**Gap:** No mention of Lazy Clause Generation paradigm that modern CP solvers use.
**From thesis:** "Many modern CP solvers are based on the lazy clause generation (LCG) paradigm"
**Impact:** Technical audience may expect this important context.

### 5. **Unclear Hall's Theorem Connection**
**Gap:** Achievements slide mentions "Hall's theorem" without explanation.
**From thesis:** Alldifferent checker "uses Hall's theorem to determine whether an alldifferent constraint is unsatisfiable"
**Impact:** This is a significant technical achievement that's unclear.

### 6. **Missing Timetable Specification**
**Gap:** Cumulative constraint explanation is too general.
